## English (en-US)

**App name (30):** TrackMode

**Subtitle, App Store (30):** Smart nutrition calculator

**Short description, Google Play (80):** Precise calorie and macro tracking for people who already know what they eat.

**Promotional text, App Store (170):** Log a meal in seconds, see calories and protein for every item, and move or copy meals between days. Recipes with cooked-weight portions included.

**Keywords, App Store (100):** calorie,macro,protein,nutrition,food log,meal tracker,diet,recipe,barcode,weight,fitness,hebrew

**Description (4000):**

TrackMode is a fast, precise nutrition calculator for people who already track calories and macros and want less friction every day.

LOG IN SECONDS
• Add foods from your own pantry, saved meals, recipes or by scanning a barcode
• See calories plus the main nutrient of every item at a glance, with fiber next to carbs
• Edit amounts inline, lower an ingredient to zero, move an item to another meal, or copy a meal to the coming days

YOUR PANTRY, YOUR NUMBERS
• Save products with per-100 g or per-serving values, units such as cups and tablespoons, and your own photos
• Build recipes and split them into portions by count, by grams, by calories or by protein, using the cooked weight of the pot
• Browse a supermarket catalog and add products to your pantry in one tap

CLEAR DAILY PICTURE
• A daily ring for calories and cards for protein, carbs, fat and fiber
• Targets that you set yourself, or suggested from your weight, height, age and activity
• A new target suggestion only when you log a new weight, never behind your back

PROGRESS AND STATISTICS
• Weight history with a trend chart, measurements and private progress photos
• TrackMode Pro: weekly and monthly statistics, macro accuracy, nutrient trends and meal distribution, plus a PDF report

BUILT FOR HEBREW AND ENGLISH
Full right-to-left Hebrew and left-to-right English, a dark theme by default, and large touch targets.

PRIVACY
Your log belongs to you. No ads, no selling of data, and you can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

TrackMode is a nutrition tool, not medical advice. Consult a professional before making significant changes to your diet.

**What's new (first release):** First release of TrackMode.
